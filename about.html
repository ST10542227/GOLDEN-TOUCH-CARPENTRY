About our Business

About Golden Touch Carpentry

Crafting quality custom woodwork, home renovations, and structural solutions across Gauteng.

Our Story & Heritage

Founded in 2020 by Sikhumbuzo Mbatha in Johannesburg, Gauteng, Golden Touch Carpentry was built on a foundation of master craftsmanship, precision engineering, and dedicated service.

Mission Statement

Our Mission is to deliver exceptional craftsmanship, reliable service, and customized carpentry solutions while actively supporting community growth and job creation across Gauteng.

Vision Statement

Our Vision is to become one of Gauteng’s most trusted and recognized carpentry providers, known for sustainable practices, durable solutions, and modern woodworking techniques

Who We Serve

Golden Touch Carpentry serves a diverse client base across Johannesburg, Gauteng, and surrounding areas:

  • Homeowners: Seeking custom furniture, kitchen cabinets, built-in wardrobes, doors, windows, roofing, and renovations.
  • Small Businesses: Requiring shop fittings, office furniture, shelving, and reception desks.
  • Property Developers & Contractors: Needing skilled carpentry for new residential and commercial building projects.
  • Institutions: Schools, churches, and community organizations requiring furniture, repairs, and shelving.
  • Landlords & Property Managers: Needing maintenance, repairs, and property renovations.
